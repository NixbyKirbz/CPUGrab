# Assets

The user-supplied official CPUGrab logo is preserved at `public/brand/logo.png`, without changing its proportions. The supplied campus banner is preserved at `public/brand/banner.png`. Home-screen icons place the same logo proportionally on a white square canvas.

The poster and student/seller flow images were used as visual and workflow references, not as application screens. Navy `#0B2257`, gold `#FFB800`, simple white controls, menu preview rails, bottom navigation, grayscale availability and pencil-only edit mode follow those references.

All location, stall and food photography below is **illustrative** stock photography from Unsplash. It is not verified as a photograph of the named dish, a CPU building or a real campus stall. Demo labels must remain until a campus administrator replaces the content with verified data.
